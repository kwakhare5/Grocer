# Gumloop — Live Design Extraction

Source: `https://www.gumloop.com/home`, inspected 2026-11-04 at 1440×1000 in headless Chrome. This is a visual and structural reference for the Grocer landing. It is not a copy of Gumloop's text, images or brand assets.

## 1. Character

Light, product-led SaaS. White canvas, near-black type, black primary buttons, thin gray borders, large product UI frames, generous vertical rhythm. Colour is mostly reserved for small diagram accents, charts and mascot illustrations. One near-black "controls" band breaks the white page. The footer is white, not black.

## 3. Type

- Display: Gellix, 48px / 500 / tight tracking for the hero; 36px / 500 for section headings.
- Body: Geist Sans, 16px / 400; nav 14px / 500; captions 12–13px.
- Headings are left-aligned, often two lines, max-width about 18–22 characters.
- Grocer keeps Fraunces (display) and Golos Text (body), the same size scale, and no italics.

## 4. Components

- **Nav:** 64px tall, white, sticky, logo left, 5–7 links center, text button + black primary button right.
- **Hero:** two columns. Compact pill above the H1, 48px headline, one support line, black + ghost buttons, then a large bordered product frame with a top bar and realistic UI inside.
- **Logo / proof strip:** small heading, six bordered logo cards, then two very large statistics with one-line captions.
- **Feature split:** copy on one side, product frame or diagram on the other; alternating sides.
- **Bento:** 3-column grid with mixed spans; each card holds a small UI figure on a light gray panel, then a 20px title and one body line.
- **Router diagram:** large light panel, faded input pills on the left, a black pill in the center, tool rows on the right, dashed connectors, one green check.
- **Dark controls band:** near-black; label + 36px heading; large chart card and two stacked cards on the right; a row of 4–5 small control features underneath.
- **Testimonial grid:** 2×2 bordered cards with logo, quote and attribution.
- **Changelog:** a top rule with a small colored square per card; title, body, small date.
- **Final CTA:** light gray rounded panel, a row of five tiny colored mascot icons, centered 36–44px headline, body, black button.
- **Footer:** white, five or six link columns, black logo at bottom left, legal and copyright on the right.

## 6. Grocer use (current 2026-11-04)

- Use Gumloop only for the principle: white/gray canvas, black primary buttons, small color marks, one dark band, no large color washes.
- Hero: WhatsApp chat sample, black connect CTA, ghost how-it-works.
- Connect: surface gray band, black submit, blue step markers.
- Features: eight ruled rows with colored numbers and small bright underlines in blue, green, orange, pink and red; no decorative cards. Below them, a working **Try a message** panel with five real replies and a soft-blue selected chip.
- How: three steps with green markers.
- Demo: plain video stage, no card frame or play icon. Recording state until `public/demo.mp4` exists.
- Safety: one dark band and a white paper bill with a red Sample stamp; no cream or yellow fill.
- FAQ: blue plus signs and section dot.
- CTA: surface gray panel, five colored mascots (pink, blue, orange, green, red), black button.
- Blue is the logo, focus ring and small accent. No blue buttons, no violet, no yellow.
